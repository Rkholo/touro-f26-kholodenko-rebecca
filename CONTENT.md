# Calendar Hub Content Inventory

## Primary user story

As a busy parent, I want to send reminders to my family so they remember events and can make themselves available.

## User flow

1. Understand what Calendar Hub does.
2. Explore the features.
3. Learn how to coordinate a family event.
4. Sign up to get started.
